# Problem Set 2

Read the following [questions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EDBPW_BsmYkT4zqJFmYy8q6-lbfTCh7X5qNfys3Xksk/edit?usp=sharing)

Answer briefly, usually in 1 or 2 sentences. Justify your choices.

Write your answers by hand on paper. The goal is to encourage you to slow down,
organize your thoughts, and explain concepts in your own words. Research on
college note-taking
[[1]](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0956797614524581) suggests
that taking and reviewing handwritten notes can support learning and retention.

This assignment is graded for completion. Make a thoughtful attempt at every
question; your answers do not need to be perfect.

Scan your **handwritten** pages and submit your answers on
[Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1360202/assignments/8913599/).
